## Design Specificity Verdict

Specific in its visual language, generic in its behaviour. The three-field system, the anchor used three distinct ways, Fueguín in three poses matched to three jobs, and the Rioplatense copy voice are all authored for this product. But the home sequence is timetable, withheld list, address, Q&A, links. No section argues why to come.

Deterministic scan: 34 findings, all false positives. 16 matched the font family name inside the embedded base64 `@font-face` blob. 1 pointed at `.regla`, a heading underline, not the rounded-card accent-border trope. The remaining 18 live in the compiled payload on lines that are CSS reset and JavaScript comments belonging to the canvas editor. The detector also ran degraded (regex fallback, missing parsers), so it is an undercount, not a clean bill of health.

Visual overlays: not possible. Artboards render inside nested sandboxed iframes with an opaque origin. The browser-evidence pass additionally reported content that does not exist in any source file and was discarded.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Mobile cannot convert
`Mobile.dc.html:142` renders day tabs as `<div>`; the file contains zero `pick` handlers. The schedule is frozen on Saturday and is unreachable by mouse, keyboard or screen reader. Mobile is also a reduction: no header, no nav, no CTA until roughly 2600px of scroll. The Ubicación data grid (including Accesibilidad) and four of six FAQ entries are cut, among them the loneliness answer and the "if cost is a problem, write to us" answer.
Fix: `.tabm` becomes `<button role="tab" aria-selected>` with a handler; sticky bottom bar with Inscribirme; restore the Ubicación `dl` and all six FAQ entries; treat mobile as the primary artboard.
Command: /impeccable adapt

### [P0] The footer's primary CTA is invisible
`.cta` hardcodes `background: var(--tinta)` with a tinta border, and `Footer.dc.html:171` places it on `campo-tinta`. Measured 1.00:1. Only `:hover` reveals it, and touch has no hover. It is the terminal CTA of the page and the only CTA in the mobile footer.
Fix: make `.cta` field-aware via `var(--regla)`, or use `.cta-fuego` in every tinta context.
Command: /impeccable polish

### [P1] Typographic regression in four artboards
`Productos`, `ProductoDetalle`, `PreSave` and `Ritmo` lack the `font-synthesis: none` block the other ten carry, so Cayento (single weight) is fake-bolded at 82px and 56px. Same fork left those four with `.cta-verde { border-color: var(--verde) }` while the other ten carry `var(--dato)`. `cta-tinta`, used in `Cronograma.dc.html:156`, is undefined in every kit revision.
Fix: move the typographic discipline block into `_kitB.css`, rebuild the four artboards, define or remove `cta-tinta`.
Command: /impeccable polish

### [P1] Every focus indicator on a light field fails
Measured against WCAG 2.2: green focus ring on paper is 1.11:1 (schedule tabs and the native `<details>` accordion); orange focus ring on paper is 2.73:1 against a 3:1 requirement, covering `.cta`, `.btn-linea`, `.chip`, `.thumb`, `.talle-btn`; `.eyebrow` at 13px is 2.73:1 against 4.5:1 across seven artboards; mobile schedule times at 17px bold are 3.13:1 on the white card while the 22px desktop version passes.
Fix: one field-aware `--foco` token; darken the eyebrow to ink and carry the accent with a rule or dot.
Command: /impeccable audit

### [P1] Pre-save asserts a fact about the visitor it cannot know
`ProductoDetalle` ships State A hardcoded ("Ya estás inscripto"), while the site has no login and `PreSave` documents that blocker honestly. Compounding: "Cambiar talle" is wired to clear the saved state, and the confirmation binds live state so pressing another size silently rewrites it to a size never submitted.
Fix: State B is the default until identity is resolved; rename the button to cancel; freeze the size at save time.
Command: /impeccable harden
